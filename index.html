WHO I AM

About me

Hello, I'm Abul Bashar Mohammad Mahi, a tech enthusiast and social media security and recovery specialist from Natore, Bangladesh.

My main focus is helping individuals, creators and e-commerce businesses protect their digital identity. I secure accounts and pages, work on recovery cases through official platform channels, and handle content moderation.

Alongside that, I am a freelance content writer and blogger. I love telling stories through words and visuals, and I explore photography, videography and video editing to create content that people remember.

I run OrveXis Media, a digital-first creative agency, and I am studying Computer Science and Technology at Pabna Polytechnic Institute. My goal is to work at the meeting point of technology and creativity, helping brands grow safely and honestly online.
